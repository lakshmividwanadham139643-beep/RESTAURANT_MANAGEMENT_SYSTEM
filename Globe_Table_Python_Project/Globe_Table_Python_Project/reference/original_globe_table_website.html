Our kitchen story

Globe Table began with one idea: Guntur should be able to taste the whole world without leaving town. Our chefs cook Andhra, Indian, Italian, Chinese, Thai, Japanese, Mexican, Middle Eastern and many more cuisines, while sensors keep every fridge and tandoor at the right temperature, so each plate tastes the way it should.

Balanced Diet Plans

Everyday meal ideas with variety and balance. Choose a plan that suits your preferences.

Food guide
🥗

Vegetarian Balanced Plan

A flexible mix of grains, pulses, vegetables, fruit and dairy or fortified alternatives.

Sample day
  • Breakfast: Idli, sambar and fruit
  • Lunch: Rice, dal, vegetable curry and curd
  • Snack: Nuts or seasonal fruit
  • Dinner: Chapati, paneer/tofu and vegetables
🍗

Non-Vegetarian Balanced Plan

Includes protein choices alongside vegetables, whole grains and fruit.

Sample day
  • Breakfast: Vegetable dosa and sambar
  • Lunch: Rice, chicken/fish curry and vegetables
  • Snack: Yogurt and fruit
  • Dinner: Chapati, dal and egg or fish
🌾

High-Fibre Meal Plan

Focuses on whole grains, beans, vegetables, fruit and adequate fluids.

Sample day
  • Breakfast: Oats with banana
  • Lunch: Brown rice, chana and greens
  • Snack: Guava or apple
  • Dinner: Millet roti, mixed vegetables and dal
🥛

Protein Variety Plan

Spreads protein-rich foods across meals using plant and/or animal options.

Sample day
  • Breakfast: Pesarattu and chutney
  • Lunch: Rice, rajma and vegetables
  • Snack: Milk/soy drink or roasted chana
  • Dinner: Paneer/tofu or fish with roti and salad
Important: These are general meal ideas, not medical or weight-loss prescriptions. Food needs differ by age, activity, allergies and health conditions. Ask a qualified dietitian or healthcare professional for individual advice, and do not skip meals or follow restrictive plans.
